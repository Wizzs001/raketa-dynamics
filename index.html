01 / THE APPROACH

Detail matters.

Raketa Dynamics creates highly detailed aircraft replicas for collectors and aviation enthusiasts, with an emphasis on accurate surface detail, weathering, cockpit reproduction and presentation.

These are display pieces designed around the aircraft itself — its configuration, markings, surfaces, equipment and character.

03 / COLLECTOR EDITIONS

Silverline / Goldline

Two collector-focused editions built around the same emphasis on detailed aircraft representation.

01
SILVERLINE

Display focused.

A highly detailed 1:72 collector model designed for display, with detailed cockpit reproduction, surface detail, weathering, markings and presentation.

  • Detailed cockpit
  • Accurate surface detail
  • Weathering and markings
  • Display base
  • Pilot figure where applicable
  • Collector materials
02
GOLDLINE

Maximum presentation.

The higher-end collector edition, adding removable components, additional equipment and enhanced presentation features where applicable.

  • Detailed cockpit
  • Removable engines where applicable
  • Removable panels where applicable
  • Detailed equipment bays
  • Additional figures and equipment
  • Enhanced display presentation
