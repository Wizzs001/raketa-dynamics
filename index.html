01 / THE APPROACH

Detail matters.

Raketa Dynamics creates highly detailed aircraft replicas for collectors and aviation enthusiasts, with an emphasis on accurate surface detail, weathering, cockpit reproduction and presentation.

These are display pieces designed around the aircraft itself — its configuration, markings, surfaces, equipment and character.
